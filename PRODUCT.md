# Product

## Register

product

## Users

BrowseLoom serves Chromium users who keep many tabs and bookmarks while researching, developing, writing, or coordinating projects. They return to the new-tab page frequently and need to scan, find, switch, group, save, and close browsing resources without learning a complex workspace.

## Product Purpose

Provide a fast, local-first control surface for open tabs, browser tab groups, bookmarks, saved sessions, and later-reading items. Success means users can understand their browsing state at a glance, act with confidence, and recover from mistakes without sending browsing data to a server.

## Brand Personality

Calm, tactile, precise. The interface may use restrained frosted materials and responsive motion to make state changes understandable, while content and familiar browser actions remain more prominent than decoration.

## Anti-references

- The current wall of equal-weight cards, outlined buttons, badges, and controls competing for attention.
- Decorative glass on every content container, aurora gradients, glowing controls, or other generic AI-generated dashboard motifs.
- Neon gaming dashboards, ornamental animations, or dense professional workbenches that make a new-tab page feel difficult to learn.
- Themes that change the product vocabulary instead of expressing one coherent interface system.

## Design Principles

- Put tab titles, favicons, and groups before product chrome.
- Use depth to explain hierarchy, not to decorate every object.
- Make motion brief, interruptible, and directly tied to state or feedback.
- Reveal risky and low-frequency actions progressively while keeping common actions immediate.
- Preserve local-first behavior and make the scope of bulk actions explicit.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Preserve complete keyboard operation, visible focus, non-color status cues, and system preferences for reduced motion. Provide more opaque fallbacks where reduced transparency or stronger contrast is needed.
